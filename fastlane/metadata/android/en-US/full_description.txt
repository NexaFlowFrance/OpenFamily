OpenFamily is an open-source, self-hosted family organizer — a private alternative to apps
like Cozi or FamilyWall. Your family's data stays on your own server.

This app connects to your own OpenFamily server: on first launch, just enter your server's
address (the same one you use in your browser, e.g. http://192.168.1.10:3001, or your
HTTPS / Tailscale address). The app hosts nothing itself.

Features:
• Shopping list with categories, prices and an aisle-by-aisle store mode
• Tasks with recurrence, family assignment and a kids' chores → allowance mode
• Shared calendar with reminders and iCal export
• Weekly planning (work & school timetables)
• Recipes and a weekly meal planner
• Household budget with charts and per-category limits
• Family profiles with health info and emergency contacts
• Real-time sync across devices, English & French interface
• Optional kiosk display for a wall tablet / fridge screen
• Connects to Mealie, Tandoor, Home Assistant, Grocy and Nextcloud

Open source (AGPL-3.0). No ads, no tracking, no third-party servers.
